BOUDOIR WEBSITE: JOBS TO GET MORE CLIENTS
Started 19 September 2026

What this file is
A running list of the things that will bring more women to the site and turn more
of them into bookings. Add to it whenever an idea comes up. Tick things off as
they are done, and write the date next to them so we can see what worked.

Where the site stands right now
The site is live and fast. It sits around position 3 to 4 on Google for
"boudoir photography Auckland". Over the last three months it got 236 clicks
from Google out of 5,880 times it was shown. The technical side is in good shape.
The reviews are on the site. The Google listing is being fed again.
What is missing now is reach. The site only has 10 pages, and a woman searching
"what do I wear to a boudoir shoot" finds a competitor's page, not yours.

DO FIRST

1. Write the pages that answer what women type into Google before they book
   This is the biggest thing left. The site has 10 pages. Each new page is
   another way in. Best ones, in order:
   - What to wear to a boudoir shoot   (the number one worry, and the most
     searched of all of these)
   - Bridal boudoir in Auckland        (women with a date and a reason to book.
     Right now this is one answer buried in the FAQs, not a page)
   - What happens on the day, start to finish
   - Gift vouchers
   If we don't: you keep showing up in searches and not getting the click. The
   site was shown 5,880 times and clicked 236 times. The pages are the fix.

2. Check the five pages Google still has not read
   Google knows the maternity, FAQs, reviews, privacy and terms pages exist but
   has never fetched them. A recrawl was requested on 17 September.
   If we don't: maternity is a whole side of the business earning nothing from
   search, and the reviews page cannot help you rank if Google has not read it.


NEXT

3. Gift vouchers, pushed at the right time of year
   The voucher offer is on the pricing page. It needs its own page and its own
   push before Christmas, Valentine's Day and Mother's Day.
   If we don't: vouchers sell themselves at those three moments and sit still
   the rest of the year. Missing the window costs a whole season.

4. Make it easier to get in touch than filling in a form
   Some women will not type their name into a form about boudoir photos. A text
   or phone option, clearly marked as discreet, would catch them.
   If we don't: we lose the most nervous enquiries, who are often the ones who
   most want to do it.

5. Follow up enquiries that go quiet
   Right now an enquiry that does not reply is lost. A short second message a
   week later would bring some of them back.
   If we don't: you are spending time on enquiries and then letting half of
   them drift.


LATER

6. Referral offer for past clients
   Women who have done a shoot are your best salespeople. Something small for
   the friend who comes, and something for the woman who sent her.

7. Social posts pointing back to the site
   Every post wants a reason to click through to the site, not just sit on
   Instagram.

8. Watch the new Australian competitor
   A bigger Australian company is moving into the Auckland market with more
   money to spend. The SEO check runs fortnightly and will show if they appear.
   We cannot outspend them. We can be the one with real reviews, real answers
   and a faster site.
