DO FIRST

1. Bridal photos
   The bridal page is live and using ordinary boudoir photos as stand-ins. None
   of those women is described as a bride, so nothing on the page is untrue, but
   it is the weakest page on the site because of it.
   Next bridal client who is happy for her photos to be used, shoot a few with
   that page in mind. A veil, a white set, the shoes, the garter.
   If we don't: the page still works, it just does not sell the way it could.

2. Push the gift voucher page before Christmas
   The page is live and it is the right time of year. It needs pointing at:
   a post on the Google listing, something on Instagram and Facebook, and a
   mention to past clients.
   If we don't: Christmas is the single biggest voucher window of the year and
   it closes in December. Missing it costs a whole season.

3. Check the new pages are being read by Google
   Several pages have gone up fast. Google needs to crawl them, and the ones
   added in September were slow to be picked up.
   If we don't: a page Google has not read earns nothing.


NEXT

4. Make it easier to get in touch than filling in a form
   Some women will not type their name into a form about boudoir photos. A text
   or phone option, clearly marked as discreet, would catch them.
   If we don't: we lose the most nervous enquiries, who are often the ones who
   most want to do it.

5. Follow up enquiries that go quiet
   Right now an enquiry that does not reply is lost. A short second message a
   week later would bring some of them back.
   If we don't: you spend time on enquiries and then let half of them drift.

6. Keep the blog going
   Two posts is a start, not a blog. One a month is plenty. Every post is
   another way in from Google.


LATER

7. Referral offer for past clients
   Women who have done a shoot are your best salespeople. Something small for
   the friend who comes, and something for the woman who sent her.

8. Social posts pointing back to the site
   Every post wants a reason to click through to the site, not just sit on
   Instagram.

9. Watch the new Australian competitor
   A bigger Australian company is moving into the Auckland market with more
   money to spend. The SEO check runs fortnightly and will show if they appear.
   We cannot outspend them. We can be the one with real reviews, real answers
   and a faster site.
